# Responsible Interpretation

This project analyzes reporting records, not verified physical events.

## Do Not Infer

- Correlation does not establish causation.
- State-level counts do not establish physical proximity.
- Reporting frequency does not equal underlying event frequency.
- A statistically detectable pattern does not verify a reported phenomenon.
- Synthetic demonstration results do not describe the real world.
- User-supplied results cannot overcome limitations in the supplied source data.

## Current Scope

The v1 pipeline supports U.S. state-coded records and first-pass validation, descriptive analysis, reporting-delay analysis, and temporal statistics. It does not provide population normalization, international geography, military or nuclear proximity analysis, or narrative analysis.

## Required Disclosures for Future Research

Any substantive finding should report the dataset type, sample size, date coverage, geographic coverage, join coverage, missing-versus-zero policy, normalization method, confounders, sensitivity analyses, exact comparison periods, and whether the result is descriptive or inferential.
